Computational research using density functional theory (DFT) to investigate the electronic structure and energetics of molecular and material systems, with applications to catalytic processes such as nitrogen fixation and water splitting. Calculations are performed using SPARC and the Atomic Simulation Environment (ASE) on Georgia Tech's PACE high-performance computing cluster.

Research conducted through the Big Data and Quantum Mechanics Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) program with the Medford Group at the Georgia Institute of Technology.
